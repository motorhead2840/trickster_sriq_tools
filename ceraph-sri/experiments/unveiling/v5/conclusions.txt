RECOMMENDATION
--------------
A programmer-declared Ξ_crit=0.9 is a usable on-tile setpoint for the selected pointer. In the Markov depolarizing model the predicted threshold r* = (Ξ_crit − 1/d)/(1 − Ξ_crit) rises slowly with d (selecting 1 of 8 needs a slightly higher rate ratio than 1 of 2). Against a T1/T2-type veil of the same Γ_eff, a pseudomode memory register helps at d=2 (slow κ ΔF=+0.105); helps at d=8 (slow κ ΔF=+0.059). If only a memoryless veil is present, a fixed γ_sel = 1.15 r* Γ0 holds Ξ_crit until Γ drifts; an oracle that tracks Γ(t) is the reference, and a Mehra-style rule that sees only a weak readout of F is the on-tile option — see the tables for whether it actually recovers. On a 2–3 tile array this run is too small to claim a wiring law; treat Experiment 4 as a smoke test.

PLAIN FAILURES AND LIMITS
-------------------------
- At d=2 the slow-κ pseudomode did not show a clear BLP backflow above the Markov baseline (N_slow=6.912e-04, N_Markov=0.000e+00). Memory is not automatically visible at this n_max / grid.
- F>0.99 was not reached at any r on the grid (max r=16). That is expected from the closed form F_ss=(r+1/d)/(r+1): d=2: F_ss(r=16)=0.971, need r>49.0; d=3: F_ss(r=16)=0.961, need r>65.7; d=4: F_ss(r=16)=0.956, need r>74.0; d=8: F_ss(r=16)=0.949, need r>86.5. The grid never entered the F_ss>0.99 region, so this is not a finite-time integration failure.
- On a stationary veil Mehra chatters around Ξ_crit at d=2 (late F=0.902, viol=0.41) because the innovation setpoint is exactly Ξ_crit and the readout is noisy; the oracle's 1.15 margin avoids this.
- On a stationary veil Mehra chatters around Ξ_crit at d=8 (late F=0.898, viol=0.47) because the innovation setpoint is exactly Ξ_crit and the readout is noisy; the oracle's 1.15 margin avoids this.
- Mehra did not hold a sustained F≥Ξ_crit through the Γ ramp at d=2 (late F=0.876, viol=0.97, rec_t=nan); it beat a fixed rate on late F but still undershot the oracle.
- Mehra did not hold a sustained F≥Ξ_crit through the Γ ramp at d=8 (late F=0.876, viol=0.98, rec_t=nan); it beat a fixed rate on late F but still undershot the oracle.
- N=2 mismatch: even with tile 0 off-target, ring vs all-to-all and J=0 vs J=1.5 changed F0 by less than 0.01; wiring did not matter at this size and coupling.
- N=3 mismatch: even with tile 0 off-target, ring vs all-to-all and J=0 vs J=1.5 changed F0 by less than 0.01; wiring did not matter at this size and coupling.
