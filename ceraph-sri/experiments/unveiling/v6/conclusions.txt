Wiring verdict: Fibonacci circulant is roughly even with torus at N=100 (ΔR̄=+0.010, ΔMSE=+8.79e-05). mod-13 circulant is roughly even with torus at N=100 (ΔR̄=+0.002, ΔMSE=+5.80e-05). Naive mod-13-only routing is a much worse layout than a degree-4 ring at N=100 (R̄=8.332 vs 1.756). AA-weighted hoppings at N=100: near-vanishing λ raised R̄ from 1.831 to 2.362 and cut λ₂ from 0.0188 to 0.0145. Weak links worsen the layout term; at 20 dB the measured MSE is still mostly the floor (8.86e-04 vs 1.02e-03) and does not cleanly rank them. Fibonacci-weighted hoppings at N=100 raised R̄ vs the uniform degree-4 ring (2.918 vs 1.756, λ₂ 0.0118 vs 0.0197); measured MSE at 20 dB was 7.06e-04 vs 7.95e-04, floor-dominated. At 20 dB the 1/SNR² layout term is small next to the floor, so R̄ and λ₂ are the reliable layout comparison; measured MSE with 8 trials is too floor-heavy to declare a winner among well-connected graphs.

RECOMMENDATION
--------------
Under strong technical noise (V_n/V_s=16) at η=0.85 and r=1, put the squeezing on the reference mode when k is inside (0.040, 0.952); put it on the signal mode outside that interval, in particular for k≥1. Advantage over the unsqueezed baseline is strictly positive at every η in (0,1] but becomes small at high loss; quote the relative-advantage table, not a slogan. This is a Gaussian-estimator fact in the stated model, not a hardware result. On wiring: do not assume that a quasiperiodic or localizing layout helps unveiling; weak links raise R̄(G) and can hurt the consensus used to recover relative veils. Fibonacci circulant is roughly even with torus at N=100 (ΔR̄=+0.010, ΔMSE=+8.79e-05). mod-13 circulant is roughly even with torus at N=100 (ΔR̄=+0.002, ΔMSE=+5.80e-05). Naive mod-13-only routing is a much worse layout than a degree-4 ring at N=100 (R̄=8.332 vs 1.756). AA-weighted hoppings at N=100: near-vanishing λ raised R̄ from 1.831 to 2.362 and cut λ₂ from 0.0188 to 0.0145. Weak links worsen the layout term; at 20 dB the measured MSE is still mostly the floor (8.86e-04 vs 1.02e-03) and does not cleanly rank them. Fibonacci-weighted hoppings at N=100 raised R̄ vs the uniform degree-4 ring (2.918 vs 1.756, λ₂ 0.0118 vs 0.0197); measured MSE at 20 dB was 7.06e-04 vs 7.95e-04, floor-dominated. At 20 dB the 1/SNR² layout term is small next to the floor, so R̄ and λ₂ are the reliable layout comparison; measured MSE with 8 trials is too floor-heavy to declare a winner among well-connected graphs.

PLAIN FAILURES AND LIMITS
-------------------------
- Layout mod13_naive is disconnected at N=52 (λ₂=-7.14e-16).
- At η=0.30 the relative advantage of squeeze-B over unsqueezed is small (max 0.016 on the scanned k); a strictly positive gap is not a large one.
- Localization in the on-site Aubry–André Hamiltonian is not the same object as a
  poorly wired comparison graph; we used weighted hoppings as the unveiling analogue
  and kept the on-site IPR as a separate diagnostic.
- A known extra coherent displacement at matched total photon number does not change
  the Wiener MSE in this model; we therefore did not invent a brighter-classical
  baseline that rescales s and n.
