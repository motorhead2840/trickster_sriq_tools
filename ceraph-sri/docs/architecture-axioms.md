## Theorem Zero — Observable Antithesis

### Informal claim

Yoga–Buddhi is an algebraic identity when \(\xi\) is known. For SRI to be an architecture (not an oracle demo), there must exist an **observable** construction of an antithesis such that the projector recovers \(v\) with a **stated error bound**.

### Formal statement (working)

**Setup.** Let the thesis observation be a random element \(Y\) in a measurable space \((\mathcal Y,\mathcal B)\), generated by latent \((v,\xi)\) under a known structural model \(\mathcal M\) (parameters may be calibrated offline). Let \(\Pi_V\) be the Vidya coordinate chart implied by A1.

**Claim.** There exists a measurable map
\[
\widehat C_S:\mathcal Y\to\mathcal Y
\]
depending on \(\mathcal M\) but **not** on latent \(\xi\) or clean \(v\), and a projector
\[
\hat\Upsilon(Y)=\tfrac12\bigl(Y+\widehat C_S(Y)\bigr),
\]
such that for a declared loss \(\ell\) and budget \(\varepsilon\),
\[
\mathbb E\bigl[\ell\bigl(\Pi_V\hat\Upsilon(Y),\,v\bigr)\bigr]\le\varepsilon.
\]

**Oracle baseline (not Theorem Zero).** With known \(\xi\),
\[
C_S^{\mathrm{orc}}(v+\xi)=v-\xi,\qquad
\Upsilon^{\mathrm{orc}}(v+\xi)=v,\qquad \ell=0.
\]
Implemented as `oracle_paired_projection` / `symplectic_project`.

**Non-goals (explicit).**
- Does not claim hardware \(\mathcal O(1)\) or Landauer circumvention.
- Does not claim a universal \(\Xi\) (see theoretical-framework §7).
- Does not claim amplitude-damping or full BLP; those remain hardening tracks.

## Three rival constructions (pramāṇas)

We treat these as competing witnesses of \(\widehat C_S\). They may later compose under A5.

### P1 — Dual-mode witness (CV MMSE)

Model \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{CV}}\):
\[
y_A=\sqrt\eta(s+n)+q_A,\qquad
y_B=\sqrt\eta\,k\,n+q_B.
\]
MMSE \(\hat s=w^\top(y_A,y_B)\) is already observable-only (`estimate_signal`).

**Antithesis reading.** Define an inferred noise mark from the reference,
\[
\hat n \propto y_B
\]
(in the linear-Gaussian case, \(\hat n\) is the conditional mean of \(n\) given \(y\)), and set
\[
\widehat C_S(y_A)\;\stackrel{?}{=}\; \hat s-\hat n
\quad\text{or act in the \((s,n)\) chart rather than raw \(y\).}
\]
**Status:** Lemma Z1 accepted — `observable_yoga_buddhi` rewrites MMSE as \(\widehat C_S\). C5 registered `open` pending runner confirmation. C1–C4 remain the sensing-model witnesses.

### P2 — Graph-ratio witness (unveiling)

Model \(\mathcal M_G\): shared-input ratios \(r_{nm}\approx\tau_n/\tau_m\) on graph \(G\). Relative \(\hat\tau\) is observable up to gauge; error-law gives MSE scaling (Track B: held-out \(R^2\approx0.80\) on the notebook grid).

**Antithesis reading.** Veil-corrected observations
\[
\tilde y_n=\hat\tau_n^{-1}y_n
\]
reduce horizontal flux *across tiles*. That is coherence restoration, not yet a single-tile \(v-\xi\) conjugate.

**Status:** necessary metrology layer (A3). Insufficient alone for A2 unless “antithesis” is redefined as *gauge-aligned field*, which must be stated explicitly.

### P3 — Selection witness (Lindblad pressure)

Model \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{sel}}\): target jumps vs depolarizing noise yield
\[
F_{ss}=\frac{r+1/d}{r+1}.
\]
Driving \(r\) above \(r^\star(\Xi_{\mathrm{crit}})\) forces population onto the declared target (Track B: machine-eps match).

**Antithesis reading.** Selection does not construct \(v-\xi\); it **contracts** unwanted support. It is a dynamical surrogate for cancellation when a target basis is declared.

**Status:** control surface (A4). Complements P1/P2; does not replace \(\widehat C_S\) unless we redefine A2 as *asymptotic support projection*, which is a different theorem.

## Lemma Z1 — locked (Gaussian dual-mode Theorem Zero form)

**Lemma Z1.**  
Under \(\mathcal M_{\mathrm{CV}}\) with known parameters \((V_s,V_n,k,\eta,r_A,r_B)\), the MMSE estimator \(\hat s(y_A,y_B)\) equals the Yoga–Buddhi image of a linear observable antithesis:

\[
\widehat C_S(y_A,y_B)=(2w_A-1)y_A+2w_B y_B,
\qquad
\hat\Upsilon=\tfrac12\bigl(y_A+\widehat C_S\bigr)=w_A y_A+w_B y_B=\hat s,
\]
with \(\mathbb E[(\hat s-s)^2]=\mathrm{MSE}_{\mathrm{MMSE}}\) from `sri.cv.gaussian`.

**Involution:** \(\hat{\mathcal C}_S^{2}=I\) on the latent \((v,\xi)\) chart only — **not** required on raw \(Y\).

**Pass criterion (C5):** algebra \(\hat\Upsilon\equiv\hat s\) (rtol \(10^{-10}\)) + sample MSE within 5σ of analytic; never receives \(s\) or \(n\).

**Provenance (2026-11-12):** SageMaker `ml.g4dn.xlarge` / Tesla T4 / JAX `cuda:0` elaborate suite — `pass_all`, Colab oracle fixture + Z1 config×seed grid green.
