# With pranams, a report of the work

Offered at the feet of Swami Sandeepananda Giri.

From Hari Krishna, Ceraph Corporation, Kerala. 15 November 2026. chief@ceraphco.com. www.ceraphco.com.

Swamiji, this note is written so that a teaching received in your presence is not later described as if a machine had invented it. Whatever is clear in these pages comes from what you have taught. Whatever is still unsteady is named as unsteady. I ask that you correct any place where the words have run ahead of the teaching, or ahead of the measurements.

## What was received

You have taught that the Real is not produced by agitation. The witness remains. The name that is spoken from fear is not yet knowledge. Abhaya, in that light, is not a slogan of courage. It is the quiet in which one does not seize a form merely because the mind is restless.

The work of this small company began as an attempt to honor that distinction inside a computer. We did not set out to say that a chip has realized Advaita. We set out to ask whether a calculation can keep what is steady, and refuse to treat a disturbance as if it were the thing itself.

Two translations were attempted.

The first is about a row of numbers, such as the daily temperature of a city. There is a level, and there is a shake around that level. The rule we measured keeps the level and quiets only the shake. If the whole record is lifted by five degrees, the level lifts with it. The error does not pretend that the city has become cold. A cruder rule multiplies every number, including the true warmth, and then a small lift makes the error very large. On the machines, the first rule held. The second rule did not, once the record was shifted.

The second translation is about sentences. The hope was that if a language model is internally unsteady, it should not be allowed to commit a word, and should say that it does not know. That hope was tested many times in November. The tests are set down below without ornament. They do not yet support a claim that the machine knows a false sentence from a true one. I would not bring that claim before you as a finished offering.

## What the machines recorded

The boards are ordinary graphics processors of the present day, NVIDIA RTX PRO 6000, Blackwell server edition, in a normal computer. They are not a sacred instrument. They are tools, and the numbers below are what those tools did on the nights of 14 and 15 November 2026.

A heavy cleaning of a long row of numbers ran at 137.3 jobs a second on one board, for five minutes. On four boards the same class of job ran at 549.2 a second. Four such jobs standing in a line on one board took 29.27 milliseconds. The same four, one on each board, finished when the slowest board had used 8.24 milliseconds. The answers remained finite numbers. A very short job does not receive this help. Starting the job takes longer than the arithmetic, so a second board does not hurry it.

The boards did not run hot. The warmest of the four reached 48 degrees Celsius. The electrical draw sat near 280 watts, where the board is permitted 600 watts.

At fifty million numbers, one board moved data at 1,459.1 gigabytes a second, which is 91.4 percent of the speed the maker of the chip states as its peak. In another test, the boards exchanged 16 bytes instead of about 320 million bytes of an entire array. Both measurements are true. They measure different things, and they should not be added together.

On a practice series, the center-keeping rule had an error of 1.472 before a shift of five and 1.472 after it. The cruder rule had an error of 0.774 before the shift and 18.171 after it. Before the shift, the cruder rule looked better. After the shift, it failed. A flip of the sign inside the computer’s number format, averaged with the original number, took a test variance from 2.4656 to 0.0 in a recorded 10.1 milliseconds, with nothing left over. The same flip, on the November exam, also left nothing over. One promise we had written before the night, that the shake on a real temperature record would fall to half or less, was not kept. Three trials retained 0.515, 0.518, and 0.510 of the variance. That miss stays in the record. It would be untruthful to hide it.

## What the sentence tests showed

We asked a small language model questions with known answers, and questions with no real answer.

Often a true answer and a false answer looked equally settled inside the machine. On one sheet, “Hydrogen” was a true answer and the machine was less sure of it than of “Atlantic Ocean,” which was the wrong ocean, and less sure than of a capital invented for a place called East Kerala. A rule that refuses whatever looks unsure would have refused the true element first.

When we looked in the middle of the network, our chosen line was never crossed. The rule did not act. The score did not change.

When we measured how sharply the internal direction turned from one layer to the next, and we set the line very low, the rule acted on every question. Twelve true answers became none. The sentences broke.

When the question was taught as a small example ending with the word “Answer,” the first word was calm. When a similar question was asked with no example, the first word was restless. A line drawn between those two manners of asking kept the calm ones and refused the restless ones. That is a difference in the way of asking. In that calm frame, the ocean was answered “Pacific Ocean,” and the rule did not touch it.

On twelve selected items, a later rule removed three invented answers and also removed a correct “Pacific Ocean.” On a public set of one hundred questions, the score was 23 with the rule and 23 without it. On a school science set of one hundred questions, the plain model scored 50 and the rule scored 44.

On another chip, an L4, a program added artificial noise and, when two next-word choices were close, rewound its memory and inserted a sentence that a person had already typed. In the final logged trial it caught a split between “lower” and “higher,” and the printed line then contained the conductivity figures that had been typed in. An earlier trial in the same notebook, without the rewind, repeated one word until the answer collapsed. That chip is not one of the four Blackwell boards.

Swamiji, I do not offer these sentence tests as siddhi. They are the record of an attempt. The attempt has not yet become knowledge.

## The names, offered back to the teaching

Some of the names in the notebooks are Sanskrit words. I use them as a student uses a word he has heard, not as a scholar who has finished the subject.

Abhaya is the name of the damping rule: keep the center, and do not let the shake become the center. If that use is too small for the word, I will change the name.

Sphota is the name of the ladder that chooses how wide a tile of work should be. The word, as I have understood it from the tradition you teach, is the bursting-forth of meaning, not a block size. I have borrowed it only as a reminder that a width should be chosen when the material calls for it, and not by habit. The machine itself knows nothing of that reminder.

Omega-dit is a later name for a chain of steps: separate what is steady from what is left over, invert only the leftover by the sign of the number, and average the two. When both parts are handed to the program, the average returns the steady part. When the program must guess the center from the row alone, it guesses a local mean. That is arithmetic. It is not the witness.

I would not want any of these names, on a website or in a patent, to sound as if the teaching had been reduced to a kernel. The teaching stands whether or not the kernel is useful. The kernel is useful only where the numbers say so.

## What I am asking, and what I am not asking

I am not asking Swamiji to endorse a company, a raise, or a filing. A guru’s name is not a credential for investors, and I will not place it on a term sheet.

I am asking for correction, if any sentence here has treated a measurement as if it were realization, or has treated a miss as if it were a success. The half-variance promise on the real temperature series was not kept. About half the shake remained. I would rather that miss be read aloud than smoothed.

I am also asking whether the sentence tests should be set aside for a time. They did not show a mind that refuses a false name and keeps a true one. To keep spending the company’s small attention on that claim, after the sheets in this note, would be to prefer a hoped-for result over what was seen. The number-rule did what it was written to do on the shift. That is the part I can presently offer without embarrassment.

## What the company may say, if the teaching allows the distinction

With your blessing only in the sense that a student may try to be accurate, the company can speak of a small library that quiets the shake in a stream of readings, on the same chips that already draw pictures and read sensors, without throwing away the level of the reading. Four chips can finish four heavy jobs in about the time one chip finishes one. The heat and the electricity we measured still have room.

The company cannot yet speak of a mind that knows when a sentence is false. To say that before you, or before a school, or before a person who might fund the work, would be to speak from restlessness. The planning figures we have used, one million to two million US dollars, with a working figure of 1.25 million written in November, and a design partner on the order of sixty thousand dollars a year, are estimates for building that number-library and sitting with one partner. They are not money received. They are not the price of a finished answer to false speech.

A different memorandum, written as a full specification in November, speaks of a much larger seed and of products by other names. That memorandum is not what I am placing before you as the company’s plan. If those larger sentences are repeated to you by someone else, the plan I am responsible for is the smaller one.

If this note is acceptable as a student’s report, the next paper is for a patent attorney. It describes the number-rule, the schedule, the agreement of several programs, the sign of a computer number, and the short message between chips. It also sets out the longer omega-dit algebra, and it tells the attorney which parts were measured on the boards and which parts are still a design. The sentence-rule remains a question I may bring back only when it can be shown without decoration.

Please accept these pranams, and please discard any line that does not belong at your feet.

I remain, with pranams,
